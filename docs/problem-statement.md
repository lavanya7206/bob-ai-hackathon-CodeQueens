# Problem Statement
L2-Supply Chain Disruption Assistant & Fleet Utilisation Optimizer

## Background
The logistics and supply-chain industry can be heavily affected by road closures, port disruptions, and weather events. A single disruption can impact multiple shipments, fleet capacity, routes, and cold-chain operations at the same time.

## The Problem
When a disruption occurs, logistics teams have to manually identify affected shipments, decide which shipments need priority, evaluate alternative routes and carriers, and find available vehicles. At the same time, cold-chain shipments may face temperature excursions, turning delivery delays into product-quality risks.

## Who is Affected
The main users affected are logistics operators and supply-chain teams who manage shipments, fleet assets, routes, and operational disruptions. They need to make quick decisions about shipment priority, rerouting, fleet allocation, and cold-chain risks.

## Why It Matters
Delayed decisions can lead to higher costs, service failures, shipment delays, poor fleet utilization, and cold-chain product-quality risks. Faster identification of risks and better allocation of available vehicles and routes can help logistics teams respond more effectively.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short
Current operations often require teams to manually identify impacted shipments, evaluate alternative routes/carriers, and match available fleet capacity under time pressure. This makes disruption management slower and reactive. ChainGuard AI addresses this by bringing disruption detection, risk scoring, route recommendations, fleet matching, and cold-chain monitoring into one decision-support system.
